Capabilities

Structure for the work between an idea and a transferable product.

We support defined pharmaceutical development workstreams and coordinate specialist partners where laboratory, manufacturing, legal or destination-market representation is required.

FormulationCMCTechnology transferPortfolio strategy
01 / Development

Formulation & CMC planning

Translate the intended product profile into a coherent technical development brief, decision sequence and evidence plan.

Scope principleLaboratory work and formal regulated activities are performed by the appropriately qualified and contracted party identified for the programme.
  • Target product profile and dosage-form requirement framing
  • Formulation-development strategy and technical risk register
  • Analytical method and specification planning
  • Stability-study strategy aligned with intended markets and product stage
  • CMC data-gap review and Module 3 workstream mapping
  • Development partner, laboratory and manufacturer briefing packs
  • Cross-functional review of technical assumptions and dependencies
02 / Transfer

Technology transfer & manufacturing readiness

Prepare product and process knowledge for disciplined handover, scale-up and execution at a selected manufacturing site.

  • Transfer scope, responsibility matrix and governance structure
  • Process knowledge, material attributes and critical parameter review
  • Source-document and batch-record requirement mapping
  • Scale-up risk identification and readiness checkpoints
  • Method-transfer and stability continuity planning
  • Technical question log, decision record and change-control support
  • Manufacturing partner coordination through defined project gates
03 / Strategy

Portfolio & product opportunity assessment

Connect technical feasibility, evidence requirements, partner availability and commercial logic before resources are committed.

  • Product concept and portfolio-fit assessment
  • Preliminary technical feasibility and development-complexity review
  • Reference-product, dosage-form and differentiation questions
  • Indicative destination-market requirement mapping
  • Manufacturer and specialist-partner screening criteria
  • Decision-ready opportunity brief with assumptions and open questions
  • In-licensing, out-licensing or co-development discussion support
04 / Coordination

Programme governance & technical coordination

Keep multidisciplinary pharmaceutical work visible, reviewable and connected to the decisions it must support.

  • Integrated programme plan, milestones and decision gates
  • RACI and accountable-owner definition
  • Risk, issue, action and dependency registers
  • Technical meeting preparation, minutes and decision records
  • Document-control and source-traceability expectations
  • Partner communication and escalation structure
  • Executive progress summaries focused on decisions and exposure
