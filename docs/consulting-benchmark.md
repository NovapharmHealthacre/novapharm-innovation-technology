# Consulting benchmark and design rationale

## Firms reviewed

The information architecture and editorial patterns were benchmarked against current official life-sciences and consulting practices from:

- McKinsey & Company — Life Sciences
- Boston Consulting Group — Biopharmaceuticals
- Deloitte — Life Sciences and Health Care
- PwC — Pharmaceuticals and Life Sciences
- EY — Life Sciences
- KPMG — Life Sciences
- Accenture — Life Sciences
- ZS — Life Sciences
- L.E.K. Consulting — Life Sciences
- Trinity Life Sciences

No copy, layouts, marks, colour systems, proprietary frameworks, or brand assets were reproduced.

## Patterns translated into the NIT platform

1. **Lead with the decision.** Large consulting firms frame the client problem before describing the offering.
2. **Connect strategy to execution.** The strongest sector practices link portfolio, product, operations, commercial, digital and organisational questions.
3. **Demonstrate a point of view.** Original insights establish intellectual authority more credibly than unverified scale claims.
4. **Use disciplined hierarchy.** Large typography, strong editorial pacing, high contrast, and purposeful interaction communicate confidence without visual noise.
5. **Make scope defensible.** Advice, programme design and partner coordination are separated from formal regulated or specialist responsibilities.

## NIT-specific interpretation

NIT is positioned as a pharmaceutical strategy and execution advisory firm with India intelligence and an international perspective. It is not represented as a global management-consulting network, regulator, law firm, laboratory, manufacturer, marketing-authorisation holder, or substitute for appropriately qualified specialists.
