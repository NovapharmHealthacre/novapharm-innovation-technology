Privacy notice.

How this website and our business-development communications handle information.

Last updated: 21 July 2026

1. Who this notice covers

This notice applies to visitors to nit.novapharmhealthcare.com and people who contact Novapharm Innovation Technology about a business or pharmaceutical programme.

2. Information you choose to provide

You may provide your name, work email, organisation, project requirements, markets under consideration and other information included in an email or business conversation.

Please do not submit patient-identifiable information, passwords, confidential credentials, payment-card information or unnecessary sensitive personal data through the website.

3. How the website enquiry form works

The website is currently a static website. The project-brief form prepares an email in your device’s email application. The form does not upload the entered content to a Novapharm Innovation Technology website database.

Your information is transmitted only when you review and send that email. It is then processed by your email provider, our email provider and the systems used to manage the resulting business communication.

4. Technical information

The website’s hosting and internet infrastructure may process basic technical request information such as IP address, browser type, requested page, date and time for security, reliability and operational purposes. We do not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural advertising technology on this website.

5. Why information is used

  • to review and respond to an enquiry;
  • to assess whether a proposed discussion is relevant to our capabilities;
  • to prepare, negotiate or perform an agreed business engagement;
  • to maintain business, legal, security and compliance records; and
  • to protect the website, our communications and legitimate business interests.

6. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers and professional advisers supporting email, IT, legal, accounting or security functions. Project information is shared with a prospective technical, manufacturing, regulatory or commercial partner only where appropriate to the discussion and subject to the agreed confidentiality arrangements.

7. Retention

Business communications are retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, engagement, legal obligations, dispute management and legitimate record-keeping. Retention varies according to the nature of the communication.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete information that you have provided, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements. You may also ask a question about how information is handled.

9. Contact

Email bd@novapharmhealthcare.com or write to Novapharm Innovation Technology, 403, R.K Plaza, Near Utkarsh School, Diwalipura, Vadodara, Gujarat 390007, India.

10. Changes

This notice may be updated when the website, enquiry process or applicable requirements change. The updated date will be shown on this page.
